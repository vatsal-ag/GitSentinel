📚 Universal Threat Archetypes & Knowledgebase

GitSentinel maps behavioral TTPs to 8 universal threat archetypes to detect both known and mutated zero-day malware.

🔒 Ransomware / Cryptor

Actively encrypts user files at high frequency, alters extensions, deletes shadow copies, and extorts victims.

Known Families: WannaCry, LockBit, Ryuk, BlackCat

Mode: ACTIVE THREAT

🤖 AI Agent Sandbox Escape

An autonomous AI agent or sandboxed container escaping containment, mounting host sockets, or spawning unauthorized subshells.

Known Families: DockerSocketEscape, LLM-PromptInject-Spawn

Mode: ACTIVE THREAT

👁️ Spyware / Surveillance ("CTRL")

Passively captures user input, keystrokes, webcam feeds, screen recordings, and extracts browser credentials.

Known Families: Pegasus, RedLine Stealer, AgentTesla, CTRL-Trojan

Mode: PASSIVE THREAT

🐛 Network Worm

Self-replicating malware scanning local subnets and brute-forcing open ports (SMB 445, SSH 22, RDP 3389).

Known Families: Conficker, Stuxnet, Mirai

Mode: ACTIVE THREAT
